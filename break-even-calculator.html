How this number is built ▾

Break-even isn't about how busy you are — it's about how many jobs it takes before a single dollar of profit shows up. Every business works the same way:

  1. Find your margin per job. Price per job minus the variable cost of doing that one job. $180 − $70 = $110
  2. Divide your fixed costs by that margin. That tells you how many jobs it takes to cover rent, insurance, admin — everything you pay whether you work or not. $4,500 ÷ $110 ≈ 41 jobs
  3. Everything past that number is profit. Job 42 onward isn't paying bills anymore — it's paying you.

If your margin per job is low or negative, raising prices or cutting job costs moves your break-even point fast — often faster than chasing more clients would.
